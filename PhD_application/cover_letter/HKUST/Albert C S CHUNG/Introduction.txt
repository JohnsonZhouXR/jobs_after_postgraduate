Albert C. S. Chung (D. Phil., Oriel College, University of Oxford, U.K.)
 

Prof. Chung received his B.Engg. degree (First-Class Honours) in Computer Engineering from the University of Hong Kong in 1995. He was an exchange student at the University of British Columbia, Canada, from 1992-93 with the Cathay Pacific Visiting Student Award. He joined The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST) in 1996 and received his M.Phil. degree in Computer Science in 1998. He then joined the Medical Vision Laboratory, Robotics Research Group at the University of Oxford, U.K. as a doctoral research student in 1998 and graduated in 2001 with D.Phil. degree in Engineering Science. Prof. Chung was a Croucher scholar (1998 – 2001) with the Croucher Foundation Scholarship. He was a visiting scientist at the Artificial Intelligence Laboratory, Massachusetts Institute of Technology, U.S.A. (Sept 2001 – Feb 2002).

 

Prof. Chung was honored to receive the British Machine Vision Association Sullivan Thesis Award for the best doctoral thesis submitted to a United Kingdom university in the field of computer vision or natural vision in 2002; the Best Ten Lecturers Award at HKUST in 2010; Best Teaching Award for MSc Program in Information Technology in 2018; and Best Teaching Award for MSc Program in Big Data Technology in 2018.

 

Prof. Chung founded the Lo Kwee-Seong Medical Image Analysis Laboratory in 2005 (2005 – 2021) with a generous donation from the K.S. Lo Foundation, Hong Kong. The laboratory focused on interdisciplinary research aiming at creating and applying engineering techniques in the area of medical image analysis, with the ultimate goal of improving the quality of healthcare in Hong Kong, China, and other regions. He is now the Director of The HKUST Research Center for Medical Imaging and Analysis.

 

Prof. Chung was the director of the Computer Engineering Undergraduate Programme, HKUST 2010-2012 (Associate director, 2008-2010). He was the UG Coordinator (2015 – 2016) and Associate Head (2016 – 2019) at the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, HKUST. Prof. Chung served as the Associate Dean of Engineering (Undergraduate Studies), School of Engineering, HKUST, between Jan 2020 and Dec 2021. In Jan 2022, Prof. Chung joined the Department of Computer Science, University of Exeter, as Chair in Artificial Intelligence (Jan 2022 – Aug 2023).

 

From Aug 2023, Prof. Chung is a Full Professor at the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, HKUST.
