1. Please provide your past experience or participation in research projects (max. 7,500 characters including spaces).
------------------------
During my master’s studies at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University (Sep. 2024 – Oct. 2025), I participated in a research project titled “Phase Transition Mechanism and Potential Function Adaptation of CsPbBr₃ under a Cross-Platform Molecular Dynamics Simulation Framework,” under the guidance of my supervisor. Focusing on perovskite material CsPbBr₃, a key candidate for optoelectronic and biomedical applications, the project aimed to reveal its phase transition mechanisms and optimize potential functions for molecular dynamics (MD) simulations. My core responsibilities included: (1) Utilizing LAMMPS (Large-scale Atomic/Molecular Massively Parallel Simulator) to build cross-platform MD simulation models, integrating AI-driven MD tools such as DeePMD and GPUMD to enhance simulation accuracy; (2) Preprocessing and analyzing simulation data (e.g., atomic trajectory, energy variation) using Python and OVITO, extracting key features of phase transitions (e.g., lattice parameter changes, atomic arrangement evolution); (3) Collaborating with the research team to adjust potential function parameters, validating simulation results against experimental data to improve model reliability. This experience deepened my understanding of integrating AI with molecular dynamics—critical for exploring biomolecular mechanisms in neuroscience—and honed my skills in scientific computing, data analysis, and interdisciplinary collaboration.
Prior to my master’s program, my 10+ years of engineering R&D experience included multiple project roles that aligned with neuroscience research needs, particularly in AI-driven image processing and system integration. As the AI-Driven Robotic Arm Project Leader at SMARTMORE CORPORATION LIMITED (May 2024 – Sep. 2024), I led the development of a dual-branch system combining “traditional hand-eye calibration + image recognition” and “Transformer-based action prediction.” Key contributions: (1) Integrated Intel Realsense/structured light 3D cameras with a 7-axis robotic arm, deploying conversational and vision models to enable real-time interaction between visual, auditory, and motor systems—mirroring the sensory-motor integration research in neuroscience; (2) Optimized Transformer models to predict robotic arm actions using joint data and task descriptions, achieving low-latency (≤10ms) responses without manual calibration, which polished my expertise in AI model deployment and real-time data processing.
In the “Wafer ID Reader” project (Oct. 2021 – Sep. 2024), a deep learning-based OCR tool for the semiconductor industry, I collaborated with algorithm teams to design end-to-end solutions from requirement analysis to deployment. The tool matched or exceeded the performance of industry leader Cognex (100% recognition rate for fixed formats vs. 99.5% for Cognex), demonstrating my ability to develop high-precision image analysis algorithms—transferable to processing neuroimaging data (e.g., two-photon imaging, fMRI). Additionally, as a collaborator on the BGI Genomics Reagent Bottle Inspection project (Oct. 2022 – Feb. 2023), I developed AI models for real-time monitoring of reagent bottle seals and OCR-based character detection on colored backgrounds, refining my skills in defect detection and low-signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) image processing—valuable for analyzing subtle neural activity patterns in imaging data.
As Co-founder and CTO of SHENZHEN PHDI CORPORATION LIMITED (Jul. 2016 – May 2020), I led the development of an Android-based car-mounted Bluetooth controller, overseeing schematic design, PCB layout, 3D printing of casings, and software programming (TI CC2540 chip). This project integrated hardware-software co-design and real-time data transmission, equipping me with the capability to develop custom experimental devices for neuroscience (e.g., miniaturized data acquisition modules). I also designed an Android-based face recognition system (Jul. 2016 – May 2017) using OpenCV, Caffe, and VGG Net, integrating BP neural networks for gender recognition and face tracking—applying computer vision to biological feature analysis, a foundational skill for processing neuronal imaging data.
Earlier, at OPT MACHINE VISION TECH CO., LTD. (Apr. 2014 – Jul. 2016), I developed 1D/2D barcode localization and recognition algorithms, achieving real-time (≤10ms) processing on low-performance i3 PCs for 2-megapixel images. I also designed rapid edge and circle detection algorithms using non-Hough fitting methods, which enhanced my ability to extract structural features from complex images—directly applicable to segmenting neurons and neural circuits in neuroimaging data.
Collectively, these projects span AI model development, image processing, molecular dynamics simulation, and software-hardware system integration, with a consistent focus on high-precision, real-time data analysis and system optimization. These experiences have equipped me with the technical toolkit to address core challenges in neuroscience research, such as neuroimaging data processing, biomolecular simulation, and experimental tool development.

1. General Field of Research
This research focuses on the interdisciplinary intersection of Biomedical Imaging, Instrumentation, Sensing and AI, Molecular, Cellular and Tissue Engineering, and Neuroscience. It aims to develop an integrated AI-driven computational platform for intelligent analysis of neural cellular/molecular imaging data and optimization of neural molecular dynamics simulations, integrating engineering technology with neuroscience research to enhance the efficiency and precision of studies on neural circuits, cell functions, and neurodegenerative disease mechanisms.
2. Specific Research Questions & Project Aims
2.1 Core Research Questions
How to design a multi-modal framework integrating AI and traditional image processing to achieve high-precision automatic segmentation, tracking, and functional annotation of neural cells (neurons, glial cells) and molecular structures (synapses, neurotransmitter receptors)?
Can a hybrid AI-traditional computing platform optimize neural molecular dynamics simulations, reducing costs while maintaining accuracy to support research on neural molecular interactions (synaptic transmission, disease-related protein aggregation) in neural tissue engineering?
How to develop miniaturized wireless sensing tools to enable real-time data feedback between in vitro neural cell/organoid cultures and computational platforms, forming a closed-loop optimization system for neuroscience research?
2.2 Project Aims
Develop a neural cellular/molecular imaging analysis toolkit supporting automatic segmentation, tracking, and quantitative analysis of neural cells and synapses with accuracy >95%.
Build a hybrid neural molecular dynamics simulation platform, optimizing GROMACS/LAMMPS via GPU acceleration and AI tuning to reduce simulation time by over 40%.
Design a sensing module for real-time monitoring of neural cell/organoid culture microenvironments (temperature, pH, neurotransmitter levels, etc.), enabling seamless integration with the computational platform.
Validate the platform’s application value in neuroscience research through case studies such as neural cell migration analysis in neural tissue engineering and molecular simulation in neurodegenerative disease drug development.
3. Research Methodology & Approach
3.1 Phase 1: Imaging Analysis Toolkit Development (Months 1-8)
Data Processing: Collaborate to collect neuron fluorescence images, neural organoid scaffold images, etc., construct labeled datasets, and perform data augmentation.
Algorithm Design: Integrate CNN (U-Net) for segmentation, Transformer for dynamic tracking, and traditional image processing algorithms; develop a QT one-click analysis interface.
Model Validation: Train models using DSC and mIoU metrics, and verify robustness on public brain imaging databases.
3.2 Phase 2: Hybrid Simulation Platform Construction (Months 9-16)
Tool Optimization: Modify GROMACS/LAMMPS source code to implement CUDA-based GPU acceleration and add custom modules adapted for neural molecular simulations.
AI Module: Build a PyTorch-based model to predict neural molecular interaction potentials, complementing traditional simulation results.
Platform Integration: Design a hybrid workflow and integrate with HPC to support large-scale computing of complex neural molecular systems.
3.3 Phase 3: Wireless Sensing & Closed-Loop System Development (Months 17-24)
Sensing Module: Develop a miniaturized sensor using STM32 and Bluetooth modules, add neurotransmitter detection functionality, optimize PCB layout, and 3D-print the shell.
Data Integration: Establish transmission protocols and develop feedback logic to adjust simulation/analysis parameters based on real-time culture microenvironment data.
3.4 Phase 4: Platform Validation & Optimization (Months 25-36)
Case Validation: Apply the toolkit to neural cell migration analysis, use the hybrid platform to study drug-pathogenic protein interactions, and verify with in vitro experimental data.
System Optimization: Iteratively improve algorithm efficiency, sensor stability, and user experience based on validation results in neuroscience scenarios.
